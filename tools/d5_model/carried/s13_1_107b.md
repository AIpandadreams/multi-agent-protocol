    Fields are concatenated **in the stated tuple order with NO SEPARATOR AT ALL.** The
    length prefixes make one unnecessary and — the load-bearing reason — make the encoding
    **INJECTIVE**: no field value can imitate a delimiter, and no absent field can shift
    the parse of the fields after it. The `0x00`-join form could promise neither once a
    field was permitted to be absent. **The rule governs EVERY span-less kind below, not
    only the one whose schema exposed the gap** — a per-kind exception is the enumeration
    shape that has failed this design repeatedly [[findings-are-classes-not-citations]] —
    and **the SAME routine serializes the tuple for FILENAME CREATION and for §13.3's
    RACE-LOSER VALIDATION**: one grammar, one renderer

    asserted** — ⛔ ⭐ **AND RECOMPUTED FROM SCRATCH IN v15, BECAUSE v14's EXAMPLE WAS
